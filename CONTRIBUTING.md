# Contributing (Engaging With Me)

This repository does not accept code contributions: it is a read-only record of private work. What it does welcome is **conversation**. This page is the portfolio version of a contributing guide: how to get in touch and what to expect.

## Ways to engage

| If you are... | The best starting point | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Hiring** for a permanent or contract role | The [role guide](docs/roles/README.md) closest to your opening | Get in touch with the role and what the team is building |
| **A potential client** with a problem to solve | [Solutions Architecture & Consulting](docs/roles/solutions-architecture.md) | Send a short description of the problem, constraints and timeline |
| **An engineer** curious about a design | The relevant [project profile](README.md#declassified-systems-catalog) | Ask about the trade-offs; I'm happy to talk architecture |
| **Reviewing** for accuracy or disclosure | [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) | Report privately as described there |

## What I can and cannot share

I can discuss architecture, trade-offs and outcomes at the level shown in these profiles. I cannot share client names, source code or anything covered by a non-disclosure agreement, and I will say so rather than give a vague answer.
