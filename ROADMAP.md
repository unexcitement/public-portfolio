> **Status legend:** ✅ Done · 🔄 In progress · 🗓️ Planned · 💭 Exploring
>
> Lines marked **✍️ To fill in** are placeholders. Everything else is drawn from what this portfolio already shows.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-10 · **Next review:** ✍️ To fill in (e.g. quarterly)
